Support

Small app. Short answers.

An app did not follow the selected microphone

Teams, Zoom, and similar apps can be pinned to a named device. Choose “Default” in that app's audio settings so it follows the macOS default selected here.

An app did not follow the preferred camera

The app sets macOS's preferred camera, but meeting and browser apps can retain a camera selected in their own settings. Choose the system or automatic camera option in that app when one is available.

Camera preview is unavailable

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and allow access for the app. Preview starts only when you ask for it and stops when its window closes.

A device is missing

Confirm it appears in System Settings → Sound. Reconnect USB or Bluetooth hardware, then choose Refresh from the app menu.

Get help

Email darkice@darkice.co or use Send Feedback inside EyeOh. Include the device type and what you expected to happen. Do not send recordings or private meeting information.
