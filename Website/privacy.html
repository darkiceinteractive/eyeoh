Privacy

The app never listens.

Last updated 10 August 2026.

The app reads the audio and camera device names and current preference identifiers that macOS exposes locally. It changes those preferences only when you choose a device.

Data collection

The app does not record audio or video, request microphone access, create an account, run network analytics, display advertising, or send telemetry over a network. Camera access is requested only when you explicitly open the local live preview, and the capture session stops when that window closes. Dark Ice receives no personal data from the app.

Anonymous statistics and crash reporting are both off by default and can be controlled separately in EyeOh Settings. If anonymous statistics are enabled, EyeOh writes a small set of operational events such as “input changed” or “preview opened” to macOS Unified Logging on your Mac. These entries contain no device names, identifiers, audio or video and are not uploaded by the current EyeOh build.

Storage

The app stores your preferred input and output volume locally, keyed to the audio-device identifier supplied by macOS. These presets are not uploaded. The chosen camera preference is managed by macOS through AVFoundation. The app does not maintain a user database. macOS and connected device drivers may maintain their own system settings under their respective terms.
