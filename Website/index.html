Private by default

EyeOh never listens or records.

It reads device names macOS already exposes, changes preferences locally, and keeps volume presets on your Mac. Camera access is used only for the live preview you explicitly open; no audio or video is recorded, saved, uploaded, or analysed.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Why didn’t Teams or Zoom change microphones?

Those apps can be pinned to a specific device. Set the microphone inside the meeting app to “Default” and it will follow EyeOh.

Does EyeOh need microphone permission?

No. EyeOh changes device settings but does not capture or monitor audio, so it does not request microphone access.

Why does camera preview need permission?

macOS requires camera permission to show a live preview. EyeOh asks only when you open Preview, stops when you close it, and never records or sends the video.

Can I use my iPhone camera?

Yes. When Apple’s Continuity Camera makes an eligible nearby iPhone available, it appears in EyeOh automatically. No separate phone app is required.

Which Macs are supported?

EyeOh supports macOS 14 or later. The public release is prepared as a universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

How do I remove it?

Quit EyeOh from its menu, then move EyeOh from Applications to the Bin. It does not install drivers or background helpers.
